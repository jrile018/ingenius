# Investment-firm systems electives

## Capability target

Map how a quantitative fund or asset manager turns research and trades into controlled positions, cash, valuation, accounting, risk, and investor-facing records, including service-provider dependencies, exceptions, and recovery.

## Selected sources

- [NYU Stern, Operating Hedge Funds](https://www.stern.nyu.edu/registrar/schedules/2015SummerMPS.html): historical advanced course covering structure, operations, administration, valuation, performance, regulation, risk, leverage, and internal controls.
- [NYU Tandon FRE-GY 7841 Hedge Fund Strategies](https://engineering.nyu.edu/sites/default/files/2023-06/FRE-GY7841_HedgeFundsStrategies_Syllabus_Fall2023.pdf): fund ecosystem and service providers, clearing/reconciliation, execution/risk, and investor relations. The syllabus is copyrighted and is summarized, not reproduced.
- [MIT 15.S08 FinTech](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-s08-fintech-shaping-the-financial-world-spring-2020/): graduate course with public videos, slides, and assignments spanning financial technologies, market participants, APIs, trading/capital markets, and regulation.
- [MIT 15.997 Advanced Corporate Risk Management](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-997-practice-of-finance-advanced-corporate-risk-management-spring-2009/pages/syllabus/): historical organizational, governance, trading-operations, and risk-control perspective.

## Synthesis and boundary

The skill produces an operating map, responsibility and system-of-record assignments, reconciliation/control records, failure scenarios, and a prioritized gap register. It is independent because this artifact is often requested without a quantitative model or code optimization task.

No course establishes current legal applicability. Whenever a conclusion depends on regulation, custody, reporting, cybersecurity, tax, or contracts, the skill must consult current primary authorities and require qualified review. It is an architecture and controls skill, not legal or compliance sign-off.
