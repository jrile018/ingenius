# Advanced elective expansion

Research date: 2026-09-26

This expansion selects advanced electives for the user's recurring work: quantitative research, execution, numerical optimization, data and distributed systems, and investment-firm architecture. It deliberately excludes foundational survey classes unless they provide a uniquely useful implementation corpus.

## Selection rules

A source must be hosted by the institution or an instructor, teach an advanced or upper-level capability, expose enough public evidence to support a workflow, and fill a gap not already owned by the existing MIT 18.642, 15.450, 15.481x, 6.172, or low-latency skills. A course is an evidence library; it becomes a separate skill only when it supports a distinct user goal, input/output contract, boundary, and evaluation set.

These skills compose by verified artifacts rather than topical similarity:

```text
empirical signal and uncertainty
    → robust decision formulation
        → execution policy and cost model

point-in-time data contract
    → research or risk output
        → operating workflow, reconciliation, and control evidence
```

The arrows are optional handoffs, not an always-loaded hierarchy. Each skill remains independently discoverable because users commonly request each outcome on its own.

## Evidence limits

Course dates and availability differ. Historical sources are retained as method evidence and labeled; they are not proof of current market structure, technology behavior, or regulation. Copyrighted source pages are linked and summarized rather than copied. Current legal, venue, vendor, and production claims require fresh primary-source verification.
