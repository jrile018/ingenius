# Systematic research validation electives

## Why this fills a real gap

The existing quantitative-finance parent already owns time-respecting empirical work. The missing layer was a stronger advanced source base for persistent time series, structural breaks, causal designs, high-dimensional inference, and high-frequency data plausibility. That is an evidence upgrade, not a new activation boundary.

## Selected sources

### MIT 14.384 Time Series Analysis

- Official source: [MIT OpenCourseWare](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/14-384-time-series-analysis-fall-2013/)
- Level/status: graduate, Fall 2013; public lecture notes and problem sets.
- Contribution: stationary and non-stationary models, VARs, frequency-domain methods, persistent-series inference, structural breaks, and likelihood/Bayesian/simulated-moment estimation.
- Use: diagnosing invalid iid inference, instability, spurious regression, and regime-sensitive forecasts.
- Limit: applications are primarily macroeconomic and the course is historical; finance translation and current tooling are synthesis.

### MIT 14.387 Applied Econometrics: Mostly Harmless Big Data

- Official source: [MIT OpenCourseWare](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/14-387-applied-econometrics-mostly-harmless-big-data-fall-2014/)
- Level/status: graduate, Fall 2014; public notes and assignments.
- Contribution: regression and matching, instrumental variables, differences-in-differences, regression discontinuity, standard errors, and high-dimensional data.
- Use: forcing a clean boundary among description, prediction, causality, and trading claims.
- Limit: causal identification assumptions do not appear merely because a financial dataset is large.

### Stanford MS&E 448 Big Financial Data for Algorithmic Trading

- Official source: [course page](https://web.stanford.edu/class/msande448/info.html)
- Level/status: advanced project course; the public page is historically dated and some course materials require Stanford access.
- Contribution: raw quotes/orders/transactions, cleaning, realistic projects, empirical plausibility beyond data mining, optimal execution, market making, and intraday modeling.
- Use: connecting research design to messy market data and operationally plausible tests.
- Limit: public project descriptions are evidence of scope, not complete methods or validated profitability.
