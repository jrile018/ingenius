# CMU 15-418/15-618 — Parallel Computer Architecture and Programming

## Scope and public material

The Fall 2026 course is active as of the research date. It publishes a syllabus, schedule and slides, Assignment 1, assignment descriptions, and project examples. Later assignment submission infrastructure is gated.

## What it contributes

The course compares shared-memory, heterogeneous, data-parallel, task-oriented, and message-passing models while explaining the hardware costs underneath them. Topics include multicore and GPU architecture, profiling, interconnects, coherence, synchronization, lock-free programming, consistency, and distributed training patterns. The assignment sequence puts pthread/SIMD, CUDA, OpenMP, and MPI against concrete workloads. Projects require proposals, milestones, reports, and explanations of measured results.

## Transfer to trading systems

Efficiency should remain distinct from raw speed. A low-latency design can consume every core and still be a poor throughput/cost trade. The skill should demand a hypothesis, per-worker evidence, and a measured explanation. Concurrent structures require separate gates for semantic correctness, progress guarantees, contention, and cache behavior. SIMD implementations must handle remainders and irregular control flow correctly.

## Limits

The current schedule can change. Only Assignment 1 is fully public and course policy requires student repositories to remain private. Historical project examples are useful patterns, not current benchmark targets.
