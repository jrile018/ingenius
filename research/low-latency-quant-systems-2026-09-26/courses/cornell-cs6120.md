# Cornell CS6120 — Advanced Compilers

## Scope and public material

The Spring 2025 self-guided course publishes videos, written notes, readings, Bril and LLVM tasks, open-source course code, and public project reports. The repository is MIT licensed.

## What it contributes

The course covers intermediate representations, local and global dataflow, SSA, LLVM, loop optimization, interprocedural and alias analysis, memory management, JIT compilation, concurrency, parallelism, and compiler construction. It supplies the semantic bridge between a proposed source transformation and the actual IR or assembly emitted by the compiler.

## Transfer to trading systems

The skill should inspect optimization remarks, IR, and final assembly when a performance claim depends on vectorization, inlining, devirtualization, aliasing, or loop transformations. Dependencies and alias assumptions are proof obligations. A legal transformation is still only a performance hypothesis until measured on the target workload.

## Limits

The self-guided version omits grading, the full course project, deadlines, and the live community. Some assigned papers may not be freely accessible. Compiler instruction alone cannot establish end-to-end system benefit.
