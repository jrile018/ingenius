# Stanford MS&E 448 — Big Financial Data for Algorithmic Trading

## Scope and public material

The archival public site contains course information, a 2021 schedule, project proposals, selected 2014–2020 reports, and resources. Core data and some lecture materials require Stanford access.

## What it contributes

The course connects high-volume quotes, orders, and transactions to empirical modeling and workplace-style projects. Public proposals address optimal execution, market making, bid/ask dynamics, realizable aggressive prices, slippage, adverse selection, fill probability, impact, and venue routing with different liquidity, latency, rejection rates, and spreads. Student reports document event-level data, full-depth books, simulator outages, nonlinear runtime, and invisible execution errors.

## Transfer to trading systems

The skill should reject signal-price fills that were not attainable. It should reconcile cash, inventory, P&L, orders, and fills after every event; benchmark deterministic full-depth replay in events per second and latency distributions; include venue rejection and latency behavior; and use chronological holdouts with transaction-cost-aware objectives.

## Limits

The site is archival and proprietary datasets/simulators are unavailable. Student reports supply realistic workloads and failure modes but are not peer-reviewed evidence for general performance claims. Public accessibility does not establish an open redistribution license; this corpus links and paraphrases.
