Use the researched MIT OCW 18.S096 and 18.642 material as a source-grounded study framework. This skill supports learning and mathematical analysis; it does not confer MIT affiliation, guarantee complete course coverage, or provide personalized financial advice.

## Shared Workflow

1. Identify the mathematical objective, supplied data or assumptions, requested depth, and whether the user wants a hint, review, derivation, or worked solution.
2. State material assumptions before applying a formula or model. Ask only for missing information that would materially change the answer; otherwise label a reasonable assumption.
3. Separate calculation from interpretation. Distinguish a mathematically valid result from empirical adequacy, causal evidence, trading profitability, or current-market relevance.
4. Verify using an independent route where practical: dimensions, limiting cases, no-arbitrage replication, covariance properties, residual diagnostics, simulation, or a second derivation.
5. Identify which claims are course-grounded, standard mathematical extensions, or current facts requiring fresh research.

## Invariants

- Keep physical probability and pricing measures distinct.
- Keep payoff expectation and arbitrage-free price distinct.
- Keep historical, conditional, and implied volatility distinct.
- Keep vector autoregression and value at risk distinct.
- Keep filtering and smoothing information sets distinct.
- Do not treat PCA directions as stable economic factors without evidence.
- Do not treat a precisely solved optimization as reliable when its estimated inputs are unstable.
- Do not infer content for unavailable lectures or student presentations.
- Preserve observation dates and information sets in empirical work; flag look-ahead and survivorship risks.
- Historical course examples, lecturer claims, and reported results are not automatically current facts.

## Boundaries

Decline to manufacture an investment recommendation, promised return, or unsupported current-market claim from coursework. For high-stakes or live financial decisions, state the educational scope and use current authoritative data only when the user separately requests and authorizes that research.

When the request is assessed coursework, follow the user's desired help level and favor diagnosis or staged hints before a full solution when their intent is unclear.
